Stroke data attribution and licence
===================================

The file mq/strokedata.py contains stroke outlines and stroke-order medians
for the 86 Chinese characters this game teaches. That data is NOT original to
this project. It is extracted, unmodified, from:

    Make Me a Hanzi
    https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi
    graphics.txt

reproduced here verbatim:
